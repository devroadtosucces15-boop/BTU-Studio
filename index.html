Desglose transparente del cálculo

¿Qué valores se suponen?

Unspecified ceiling height defaults to 8 ft. The cooling estimate uses 20 BTU/h per square foot, plus simplified occupancy, window and room-type terms, then applies sunlight, insulation and climate modifiers. Heating uses a separate heuristic. None of these is a substitute for an engineered load calculation.

¿Qué significa BTU/h?

BTU/h measures thermal power, not electrical consumption. One cooling ton is 12,000 BTU/h. Sensible cooling changes temperature; latent cooling removes moisture.
